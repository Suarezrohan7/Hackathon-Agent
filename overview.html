micro1 Frontier Engineering Challenge

Strategy Validation Agent

Give it a backtested trading strategy. It tells you whether the edge is real, overfit, or luck — and shows the out-of-sample evidence for the call.

The result

Same 13 test strategies, each with a known answer. Same task. One question: does the verdict match the truth?

Baseline — one AI call 38% 5 of 13 correct. Reads the strategy’s own flattering backtest and guesses.
The agent 100% 13 of 13 correct. Runs 7 checks on unseen data and decides from the numbers.
baseline38%
agent100%

Cost: ~$0.05 per strategy. Runtime: ~56 s. Zero errors across the run.

How it works

  1. 1
    Profile
    Read the strategy code; flag anything that peeks at future bars.
  2. 2
    Plan & run checks
    The AI runs 7 tests: out-of-sample, walk-forward, Monte-Carlo permutation, parameter sensitivity, look-ahead probe, transaction-cost stress, trade count.
  3. 3
    Verify
    A second AI pass keeps only the findings the numbers actually support.
  4. 4
    Decide
    A fixed rule turns the verified evidence into the verdict. Transparent, reproducible, can’t drift.
  5. 5
    Explain & gate
    The AI writes the plain-English reason; a “real edge” verdict needs human sign-off before anything is called tradable.

Why the jump — the lesson

The AI was the weak link in its own pipeline. It gathered the right evidence, then talked itself out of it — one strategy had every “this is real” signal and it still said no.

Moving the yes/no to a six-line rule over the evidence the AI had already verified took it from 38% to 100%. Use the model for judgement it’s good at — which tests matter, how to explain them — not for a call a rule makes more reliably.
